# Q: 我发现现在是不是采集完才进行那个10/20次迭代

## Answer

Expanded from original query via vocab: [capture, episode, admission, training, drain, update, critic, actor, online, loop, replay, cycle]. Yes: the current episode is first captured and sealed, then the operator selects success/failure, then admission writes it to replay, and only then drain-admission-budget waits for its deterministic training budget to complete. The budget is not always 10 Actor/20 Q: cycles=ceil(new TD rows/64), capped at 10; every cycle performs 2 Twin-Q updates and 1 Actor update attempt. Thus the maximum per admitted episode is 20 Q plus 10 Actor attempts; 402 admitted rows produce 7 cycles, 14 Q, 7 Actor. Candidate export occurs every 10 applied Actor steps and activation is at the next episode boundary.
